Chapter 1

My Eating Philosophy

Healthy eating doesn't require a science degree. It just requires a few guiding principles you can actually remember.

⚖️
The 80/20 Rule
Eat nourishing whole foods 80% of the time. The other 20%? Live your life. Guilt has no place at the dinner table.
🍽️
Build Your Plate
Every meal: Protein + Carbs + Vegetables + Healthy Fat. Simple, balanced, satisfying.
💧
Hydrate First
Drink 2 litres of water daily. Start every morning with a full glass before your coffee.
🏠
Cook at Home
Aim for 4 home-cooked meals per week. You control the ingredients, the portions, and the love in every bite.

Healthy eating is not about eating less — it's about eating right.

Chapter 3

My Favourite Recipes

Simple, real recipes under 30 minutes. No special equipment. No complicated ingredients. Just food that tastes good and makes you feel great.

🥣
Power Oat Bowl
⏱ Ready in 10 minutes
  • ½ cup rolled oats + 1 cup milk or water
  • 1 banana, sliced
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter or almond butter
  • Handful of berries + drizzle of honey
  • Cook oats, top with everything. Done.
🥗
Easy Protein Salad
⏱ Ready in 15 minutes
  • 2 cups mixed greens + cherry tomatoes + cucumber
  • 1 cup cooked protein of choice
  • Dressing: olive oil + lemon + salt + pepper
  • Top with seeds or avocado
  • Toss and enjoy immediately.
🍛
One-Pan Stir Fry
⏱ Ready in 20 minutes
  • 1 cup cooked rice or noodles
  • 1 cup mixed veg (broccoli, carrot, pepper)
  • 1 cup protein (chicken, tofu, or shrimp)
  • Sauce: soy + garlic + ginger + sesame oil
  • Stir-fry, add sauce, serve over rice.

Chapter 5

My Workout Philosophy

The best workout routine is the one you actually do. Not the most intense one — the one that fits your real life.

Progress, not perfection. Show up, even when it's imperfect.

📅
3–4 Days a Week
Start with 3 sessions per week consistently. Gradually build to 4. Sustainable beats intense every time.
📈
Progressive Overload
Every 2–3 weeks, add weight, add reps, or reduce rest. Small increases compound into huge results.
😴
Rest IS Training
Rest days are not failures. Sleep 7–9 hours. Your body grows during recovery, not during the workout.
🎯
Find Your Why
Move for energy, strength, and mental clarity — not just looks. Your reason keeps you going on hard days.

Chapter 7

Top 10 Gym Tips

Whether you're just starting or coming back after a break, these tips will save you time, energy, and frustration.

1
Warm up every single time — 5 minutes, no exceptions
2
Form before weight — ego lifting causes injuries
3
Track your workouts — what gets measured gets improved
4
Rest 48–72 hours before training the same muscle group
5
Eat 20–30g protein after training for muscle recovery
6
Consistency over intensity — 3 moderate sessions beat 1 brutal one
7
Sleep 7–9 hours — sleep IS training for your body
8
Find music or a podcast that genuinely motivates you
9
Celebrate non-scale victories: energy, strength, better sleep
10
Progress photos every 4 weeks — daily changes are invisible

The gym teaches you more than fitness. It teaches you discipline, patience, and how to show up for yourself.

Chapter 8

Books That Changed How I Think

Books are one of the cheapest, most powerful tools for personal growth. These are the ones that genuinely shifted my perspective.

Atomic Habits
James Clear
The definitive guide to building good habits and breaking bad ones. One of the most practical books ever written.
Small 1% improvements compound into remarkable results over time.
Why We Sleep
Matthew Walker
A science-backed exploration of sleep's role in every aspect of health. This book will change how you think about bedtime forever.
Sleep is not a luxury. It is the foundation of everything else.
Essentialism
Greg McKeown
How to do less, but better. A guide to eliminating everything that doesn't matter and focusing on what truly does.
If it's not a clear yes, it's a no.
The Subtle Art of Not Giving a F*ck
Mark Manson
A refreshing take on focusing your energy only on what truly matters. Anti-self-help, self-help at its finest.
Choose your struggles wisely. Meaning comes from what you choose to work for.
The Comfort Book
Matt Haig
A gentle, honest collection of thoughts on living well. Perfect for hard days when you just need a kind reminder.
You don't have to have it all figured out to have a good life.
The Body Keeps the Score
Bessel van der Kolk
How stress and emotions are stored in the body — and what to do about it. A profound book on the mind-body connection.
Your physical wellbeing and mental health are deeply, inseparably connected.
Chapter 9

How I Read More Every Day

20 minutes a day = 12–15 books a year. It's not about time. It's about intention.

☀️
Morning Ritual
15–20 min with coffee, before touching my phone. The most peaceful reading of the day.
🚌
Commute
Audiobooks while travelling. Never waste a commute — it's free reading time you already have.
🌙
Before Bed
Replace screen time with reading. It improves sleep quality AND gets you through more books.
📋
Keep a Reading List
Always have your next book ready. When you finish one, never spend a week deciding what to read next.

Reading is to the mind what exercise is to the body.

Bonus

Your 30-Day Balanced Life Plan

Bring together everything from Food, Gym, and Books into one gentle, achievable daily structure.

Daily Non-Negotiables

💧 Drink 2L of water
🥗 One balanced home-cooked meal
💪 Move your body (even 20 min walk)
📚 Read for 10 minutes
🛌 Sleep 7–8 hours
Week 1
Foundations
  • Set up your Sunday meal planning routine
  • Start with 3 gym sessions (beginner plan)
  • Choose your first book and begin reading
  • Track water, sleep, and meals daily
Week 2
Build Momentum
  • Try 2 new healthy recipes
  • Add progressive overload to workouts
  • Read 20 pages per day
  • Reflect on Week 1 — what worked?
Week 3
Deepen the Habit
  • Full week meal prep every Sunday
  • Add a yoga or stretching session
  • Finish your first book — write 3 takeaways
  • Share your progress with someone you trust
Week 4
Integrate & Celebrate
  • Reflect on how far you've come
  • Choose next month's book
  • Plan next month's gym progression
  • Celebrate! A habit formed is a life changed.
